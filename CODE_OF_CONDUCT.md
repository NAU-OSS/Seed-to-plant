\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Commitment



Seed-to-Plant is intended to be a welcoming and respectful open source project for students, educators, developers, and other contributors.



We are committed to creating a community where people can participate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or role.



\## Expected Behavior



Participants are expected to:



\- Communicate respectfully and constructively

\- Be patient with new contributors

\- Give feedback that focuses on the work, not the person

\- Respect different ideas and perspectives

\- Help maintain a supportive learning environment

\- Accept constructive feedback professionally



\## Unacceptable Behavior



The following behaviors are not acceptable:



\- Harassment or discriminatory language

\- Personal attacks or insults

\- Intimidation or repeated hostile behavior

\- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions

\- Sharing another person's private information without permission

\- Any behavior that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming



\## Reporting Problems



If you experience or observe inappropriate behavior, please report it through the repository's GitHub Issues or contact the project maintainer directly.



Reports should include enough information to understand what happened while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private information.



\## Enforcement



Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or participants when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.



Responses should be fair, respectful, and appropriate to the situation.



\## Scope



This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the Seed-to-Plant project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-related communication.
